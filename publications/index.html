The page also features applied R&D led through GlobeEye, using satellite data, alternative data and AI to develop environmental intelligence for finance, business and public policy, with projects covering air and water pollution, forest biomass, carbon measurement and natural assets.

Sovereign Risk and Financial Crisis

The International Political Economy of the Eurozone

Palgrave Macmillan · 2015

An international political economy analysis of how domestic and global political dynamics influence sovereign credibility in financial markets. The book examines how institutions, policy choices and market perceptions interact during periods of financial stress, offering a framework for understanding sovereign risk and financial stability.
